# Feasibility

**Verdict: go.** The data supports both models (fair value and value growth)
for both leagues, with full features from season 2015 and basic features back
to 2010. Transfermarkt values are a sound target, with one known bias in the
Süper Lig that the models have to account for.

All numbers come from `scripts/feasibility.py --from-processed`, run on
2026-10-07 against the full backfill (31 seasons, 24,372 player season rows)
and the transfermarkt-datasets snapshot (values up to 2026-06-12). The run made
0 API requests. Generated tables: `reports/feasibility_tables.md`; every number:
`reports/feasibility.json` (both regenerated by the script, not committed).

"Regular player" below means at least 450 league minutes in the season (five
full matches). Players below that are kept in the data but say too little
about their level to train on.

## 1. Which stats exist, and from when

Share of regular players with a value, by field. A field counts as usable
from the first season after which every season reaches 90%.

| Field | EPL | Süper Lig |
|---|---|---|
| appearances, minutes, goals, cards, substitutions | 2010 | 2011 |
| rating, passes, key passes, duels, dribbles | 2015 | 2015 |
| shots | 2015 | 2015 (see note) |
| tackles | 2016 | 2018 |
| assists | 2015 (see note) | 2015 (see note) |

Before 2015 only the basic counts exist: up to 2013 there is no rating,
shots, passes or tackles at all, EPL 2014 has them for about a third of
players and Süper Lig 2014 has none.

**Blank means zero, in seasons where a stat is recorded.** From 2020 onward
the API never sends an explicit 0 for shots, key passes or tackles; a player
with no shots gets a blank. Assists behave the same way up to 2022. Goals mix
both (in Süper Lig 2024: 8 explicit zeros, 135 blanks, mostly goalkeepers and
defenders). Taken at face value this makes the raw completeness look worse
than it is (Süper Lig shots dip to 89% in 2020 to 2022). The feature step will
therefore read a blank count as 0 for a player with minutes, but only in a
league season where that stat is recorded for most players. In 2010 to 2013
the detailed stats are blank for everyone, which is missing, not zero. The
adapter and the raw cache stay faithful to the source; this rule belongs to
feature building.

## 2. Linking players to Transfermarkt

Matching blocks on exact date of birth, scores names with fuzzy token sets,
and (new in this run) accepts a candidate with the same surname and first
initial when exactly one candidate qualifies. That rule recovered 79 players
the fuzzy score missed because of nicknames, among them Phil Foden, Nick Pope,
Danny Ings and Matty Cash, and Nico Yennaris, listed by the API under his
later name Li Ke.

| | EPL | Süper Lig |
|---|---|---|
| Distinct players, all seasons | 4,049 | 4,399 |
| Matched | 3,575 (88.3%) | 3,911 (88.9%) |
| Regular players matched, seasons 2015 to 2025 | 99.2% | 98.5% |
| Regular players matched, seasons before 2012 | 84.0% (2010, 2011) | 81.1% (2011) |
| Players matched to different targets in the two leagues | 0 | 0 |

The unmatched are mostly youth and squad players with a handful of minutes:
`low_score` rows are, on inspection, other people who share a birth date,
and 37 EPL and 58 Süper Lig players have no birth date in the API.
The older seasons match less often because Transfermarkt match appearances
only start in 2012/13 and some players from 2010 and 2011 have thin
Transfermarkt profiles.

**Precision.** I checked a random sample of 50 matches per league by hand,
plus all 79 surname rule matches: every one was the same player. With 179 of
179 correct, precision is very likely above 98%.

## 3. Training labels

**Fair value** (value at the season's feature date, 30 June):

| | EPL | Süper Lig |
|---|---|---|
| Regular player seasons with a value, 2015 to 2025 | 4,305 (99.0%) | 4,123 (97.1%) |
| Same, all seasons from 2010 or 2011 | 5,969 | 5,292 |

**Value growth** (a value at the feature date and again 24 or 36 months later;
the snapshot ends on 2026-06-12, so +24 months reaches season 2022 and +36
months season 2021):

| | EPL | Süper Lig |
|---|---|---|
| Player seasons with a +24 month label | 5,341 | 4,984 |
| Player seasons with a +36 month label | 4,597 | 4,087 |

These counts are on the Transfermarkt side, before linking to stats. At the
overall match rate of 88%, that leaves about 9,000 rows with stats for the
24 month horizon and 7,600 for 36 months: enough for a gradient boosted model
and a fair backtest, not for a deep model.

**Gap to watch:** Süper Lig values around mid 2025 are thinner (season 2024:
87% of regular players valued against 97% to 99% in other recent seasons).
The backtest should report that season separately.

## 4. Is Transfermarkt a reliable target?

For each paid transfer into or out of either league, I compared the fee with
the player's latest Transfermarkt value on or before the transfer date (at
most a year old). Free moves and loans (fee 0) are excluded.

| | EPL | Süper Lig |
|---|---|---|
| Paid transfers with a prior value | 2,108 | 756 |
| Rank correlation, value vs fee (Spearman) | 0.85 | 0.81 |
| Median fee divided by value | 1.22 | 0.68 |
| Fees between half and double the value | 70% | 61% |

By period, the EPL correlation is stable (0.84 up to 2014, 0.85 in 2015 to
2019, 0.86 since 2020); the Süper Lig one is 0.74 on only 40 transfers up to
2014 and 0.81 since.

What this means:

* **Ranking is reliable in both leagues.** A player Transfermarkt values
  higher almost always costs more. That is what a fair value model and an
  undervaluation flag rely on.
* **Levels differ by league.** EPL buyers pay about 1.3 times the value
  (median for transfers in), while Süper Lig buyers pay about 0.67 times.
  So a Süper Lig value is closer to a ceiling than to a price. The models
  must carry the league as a feature, and an "undervalued" flag must compare
  a player with others in the same league, never across leagues on raw euros.
* **Süper Lig values are noisier** (61% within a factor of two against 70%).
  Prediction intervals for Süper Lig players should be wider, and the
  backtest should report each league on its own.

## 5. API requests

The full season level backfill took 1,178 requests (610 EPL, 566 Süper Lig,
plus season lists), about 38 pages per season.

Match level stats (`/fixtures/players`, one request per match) would cost
about 10,700 requests for all 31 seasons: 6,096 for the EPL and 4,605 for the
Süper Lig. On the Ultra plan (75,000 a day, until 2026-11-07) that fits in a
single day. They are **not needed** for the first models, which use the 30
June feature date, but they would allow features at any date (for example
form before the January window) and per 90 rates from the 2010 to 2014
seasons. Deciding before the Ultra plan ends is cheap; after it ends it costs
about 107 days of the free tier.

## 6. Decisions this supports

1. **Feature date 30 June (D9 in the README): confirmed.** Season totals give
   99% label coverage at that date from 2015 on.
2. **Two feature tiers.** Full features from 2015, basic features (minutes,
   goals, appearances, cards, age, position, league) back to 2010. The
   backtest compares a full model trained from 2015 with a basic one trained
   from 2010, rather than imputing seasons that never had the detailed stats.
3. **Blank counts become 0** in feature building, under the rule in section 1.
4. **League is always a feature**, and undervaluation is judged within a league.
5. **Backtest by league and season**, with Süper Lig 2024 flagged.

## Open questions

* Fetch match level stats while the Ultra plan lasts? About 10,700 requests,
  one day. Recommended if per match form or January features are wanted later.
* Positions come from the API's four groups only. Transfermarkt's
  `sub_position` (for example Centre Back, Left Winger) is available for
  matched players and would make similarity search sharper.
